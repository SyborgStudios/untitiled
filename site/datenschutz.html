2. Hosting and server log files

This website is hosted by an external service provider. 3. Google Analytics

This website uses Google Analytics 4, a web analytics service provided by Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland (“Google”). Google Analytics uses cookies and similar technologies that enable an analysis of how the site is used (e.g. pages visited, session duration, approximate region, browser). Google Analytics 4 processes IP addresses only in truncated or transient form and does not store them permanently. The information collected is generally transferred to Google servers and may be transferred to the USA. Google LLC is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

In addition to page views, use of the audio player for the reading version of the screenplay is recorded: that playback was started, plus one event per minute listened – each with the language version, the position in the recording and the page. This shows whether and for how long the recordings are listened to. No personal identification is established in the process.

Analytics is used for statistical evaluation of site usage (Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR). You can prevent collection by installing the browser add-on available at https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout, disabling JavaScript, or blocking cookies in your browser.

The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6 (1) (a) GDPR); you may withdraw it at any time — via the unsubscribe link in every email or by contacting the address above. After unsubscribing, your data is deleted unless statutory retention obligations apply.

We use the service Buttondown (a provider based in the USA) to manage and send the newsletter. Your data is processed on servers in the USA. It is not shared with any other third parties.
